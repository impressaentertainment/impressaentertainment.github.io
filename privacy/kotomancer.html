KOTOMANCER Privacy Policy

ImpressaEntertainment,Inc. / Effective: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how ImpressaEntertainment,Inc. ("we") handles information in the game "KOTOMANCER" (the "App").

1. Information we collect

The mobile (Android) version of the App does not collect, transmit or share any data, including personal information. The App contains no advertising, analytics, account registration or third-party services.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, dictionary records and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or any third party. Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

3. Purchases

Purchases of the App are processed by Google Play. Payment information is handled under Google's privacy policy; we do not receive it.

4. PC version (Steam)

Online matches in the PC version use Steam and exchange only display names and in-game moves with your opponent. Steam data is handled under Valve Corporation's privacy policy.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
